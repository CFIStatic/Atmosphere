Create an account

Email and password — then you are in. If your team already uses Atmosphere, enter their join code when you sign up.

1 · Create your account

Email and password. Your password goes straight to our authentication provider — we never see or store it.

2 · Name your company

Optional. If your team already has a workspace, enter their join code instead.

3 · Pick your role and trade

Crew Lead, Field Technician, Project Manager, or Reviewer — then pick your trade from the full service contractor list.

4 · Share the join code

Every organization gets one. Hand it to a teammate and they're in — no invites, no admin console.
