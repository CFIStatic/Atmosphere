Getting started

Create an organization, bring the crew in with one code, and hand an agent its first job.

Create your organization

Sign up with an email and a password. The password goes straight to Supabase Auth — Atmosphere never sees or stores it. Immediately after your first sign-in, onboarding asks you to either create a new organization or join an existing one with a code.

The join code

Every organization gets a unique join code. It is the whole invitation system: hand it to a teammate, they enter it at sign-up, and they land in your workspace. No invites, no admin console.

Anyone with the code can join your organization — treat it like a key, and share it in your crew chat rather than publicly.

Pick a role and a kind of work

Each person chooses an account type — Project Manager, Field Technician, Accountant, Office Manager, or Sales — and the kind of work they do: Mitigation, Construction, or both. The role decides which platform their day starts in: Sales reps land in the Sales Platform, PMs in Operations, technicians in Field, and accountants and office managers in Manager.

Faster sign-in with a PIN

On a device you use daily, enroll an optional 4-digit PIN. It is bound to that one device — the server keeps only hashes, never a secret or a session token — so a stolen PIN without the enrolled device is worthless.

Forgot your password?

Request a reset link by email from the sign-in screen. The token exchange happens on the server, so no credential ever passes through the page's JavaScript.

Hand an agent its first job

Describe the task the way you would hand it to a teammate: build this estimate, enter this into the portal, pull these numbers. The agent executes, the Verifier checks the work in a read-only browser, and the run lands in your audit trail — replayable step by step.
