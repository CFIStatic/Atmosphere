Getting started

A real first day on Atmosphere Work Verification — Platform + Field Capture only: create the company, pick a plan, start a job, film in Field Capture, and see the videos land on the Evidence Platform.

1. Create the company

Go to signup (or platform.atmosphereteam.com). Enter your name, work email, password, and company name in one step. You become Global Admin. Invited teammates use the join link or code from Settings — they do not pay the bill.

Anyone with the join code can enter your organization. Treat it like a key: share it in crew chat, not publicly, and rotate it from Settings if it leaks.

2. Pick a plan

Choose the plan that matches how many Field Capture seats you need, then add a payment method:

  • Starter — $399/mo · 1 Field Capture seat
  • Work Verification — $849/mo · 3 seats (the default at signup)
  • Scale — $1,999/mo · 10 seats

Extra Field Capture seats are $125/mo each. AI usage is billed the day it runs. After billing, Atmosphere sends you straight to Start a job — not an empty dashboard.

3. Start the first job

A job name is enough. A short note and invite list are optional. Teammates you invite get a Field Capture link for that job. Outside workers can get a job-only link without joining the whole organization.

4. Film in Field Capture

  1. Open Field Capture on the phone at app.atmosphereteam.com (or the App Store build). Sign in with the same email and password as Platform.
  2. Pick the job you just created.
  3. Record. Film a wide establishing shot, then walk the work. Speak readings out loud — the mic is part of the record.
  4. Stop when that pass is done. Upload continues in the background. Film again later the same day if you need progress or after shots.

Field Capture is the camera and the upload. AI reading happens on the Evidence Platform, not on the phone.

5. Watch it land on the Evidence Platform

Back on platform.atmosphereteam.com, open the job (or Overview). Each upload shows on the job file with title and preview. Door checks cover location, time, and hash. When a scope is present, AI reads the footage against it.

What “done” looks like

A first verified day is done when:

  • At least one before and one after (or the day’s videos) are uploaded and attached to the job
  • Door checks show location, time, and hash findings
  • Dictation / narration has written what the footage shows (and scope coverage when a scope is present)
  • The job file holds the videos, the reading, and an append-only chain of custody you can share with a customer or partner
