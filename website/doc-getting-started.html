Getting started

A real first day on Atmosphere Work Verification: create or join an organization, pick a plan, open a job, film in Field Capture, and see the videos land on the Evidence Platform.

1. Sign up

Go to signup (or open the Platform at platform.atmosphereteam.com) with an email and a password. Your password goes straight to our authentication provider — Atmosphere never sees or stores it.

Immediately after your first sign-in, onboarding asks you to either create a new organization or join an existing one with a code.

2. Create or join an organization

Create: name the company. You become the first member and get the org join code.

Join: enter the code someone already on the team gave you. You land in their workspace — no separate invite email required.

Anyone with the join code can enter your organization. Treat it like a key: share it in crew chat, not publicly, and rotate it from Settings if it leaks.

3. Choose a role and a trade

Each person picks a role — Crew Lead, Field Technician, Project Manager, or Reviewer — and the kind of work they do: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, facilities, or another service trade.

Crew leads and technicians live in Field Capture on the phone. Project managers and reviewers spend most of their time in the Evidence Platform on job files, Videos, shares, and custody.

4. Pick a plan

Choose the plan that matches how many Field Capture seats you need:

  • Starter — $399/mo · 1 Field Capture seat
  • Work Verification — $849/mo · 3 seats (the default at signup)
  • Scale — $1,999/mo · 10 seats

Extra Field Capture seats are $125/mo each. AI usage is billed the day it runs. Enterprise is contact sales only — there is no self-serve Enterprise checkout. Optional Chest Mount hardware is a one-time $49.99. See pricing.

5. Optional PIN on a daily device

On a phone or laptop you use every day, enroll a 4-digit PIN after password sign-in. The PIN is bound to that one device — the server keeps only hashes, never a secret or a session token — so a stolen PIN without the enrolled device is worthless.

Forgot your password? Use the reset link from the sign-in screen. The token exchange happens on the server.

6. Create or open a job

In the Evidence Platform, create a job (address, customer, scope if you have one) or open an existing job file. The job is the file that videos attach to — same job in Field Capture and on the Platform.

If a partner or sub only needs to film one job, you can send them a job link instead of the org join code. They film and upload for that job without joining the whole organization.

7. Film the first before (and after) in Field Capture

  1. Open the Field Capture app on the phone. Sign in (or use your PIN).
  2. Confirm you are in the right organization. Pick the job from the list.
  3. Start recording. Film a wide establishing shot, then walk the work lines. Speak readings out loud — the mic is part of the record.
  4. Stop when that pass is done. Upload starts (often in the background). Multiple videos on the same job the same day are fine — morning before, midday progress, evening after.
  5. When the day’s work is finished, film the after the same way.

Field Capture is the camera and the upload. It does not run AI reading or dictation on the phone — that happens in the office on the Evidence Platform.

8. Open Videos on the Evidence Platform

On a computer, sign in at platform.atmosphereteam.com. Open the job, then the Videos list. Each upload should show its own title and preview. Open a video to watch it in the player.

At the door, the Platform runs checks for location, time, and hash before the footage is treated as evidence. When a scope is on the job, AI dictation / narration reads the video against that scope. You can also Ask about a video from the reading and transcript.

What “done” looks like

A first verified day is done when:

  • At least one before and one after (or the day’s videos) are uploaded and attached to the job
  • Door checks show location, time, and hash findings
  • Dictation / narration has written what the footage shows (and scope coverage when a scope is present)
  • The job file holds the videos, the reading, and an append-only chain of custody you can share with a customer or partner
