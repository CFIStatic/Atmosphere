Terms of service

Last updated July 31, 2026. Plain language on purpose.

The service

Atmosphere provides Work Verification software for service contractors — Field Capture for filming and checking work on site, and the Evidence Platform for recording, verifying, and storing that work in a replayable chain of custody. Both are included in one subscription because the system requires both.

Accounts and organizations

You're responsible for your account credentials and for who holds your organization's join code — anyone who enters it joins your organization. Each person gets their own login; sharing one account between people isn't allowed.

Billing

  • Subscriptions are billed monthly at $599 for the full Work Verification system (Field Capture + Evidence Platform), plus usage, as described on our pricing page.
  • Usage is prepaid. You buy credits; agents draw them down at the published rate. At a zero balance new work pauses — we never bill usage you haven't already funded. Reload automatically or manually, your choice, subject to any spend cap you set.
  • Credits are not redeemable for cash and unused credits expire only when the organization closes.

Your systems, your authorization

When you connect a portal, a supplier site, or a computer, you confirm your organization is authorized to access it and to automate that access under its terms. Agents act with the access you granted and no more; the audit trail records what they did.

Acceptable use

Don't use Atmosphere to break the law, to access systems you aren't authorized to access, or to attack the service itself. We can suspend organizations that do.

Your data, our software

Your organization owns its data, including everything agents produce for it. We own the software. You give us the limited license needed to run the service — moving your data through models, verifiers, and the record on your behalf.

Warranties and liability

Atmosphere reads footage, checks integrity, and records custody — but invoices, client submissions, and legal decisions are your organization's calls, and a person should review what goes out the door. The service is provided as-is, and our total liability is capped at the fees your organization paid us in the twelve months before the claim.

Changes and ending things

We'll give notice of material changes to these terms. You can close your organization at any time; we can end service for breach. Your record stays exportable on the way out.

These terms are a plain-language draft pending review by counsel; the mechanics they describe — prepaid credits, pause at zero, append-only records — are how the product actually works.
