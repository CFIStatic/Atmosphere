Second agent

The Verifier

What's safe to correct, it corrects. What it isn't sure about, it brings to you — with the evidence attached.

Learning layer

It gets measurably better at your work

Every run — a drafted scope, a built estimate, extracted document fields, a customer update — is scored. The routing policy improves from those scores, so the platform executes your work better this month than it did last month. And you can see it, because the scores are on the record too.

This isn't a model trained on the internet's idea of restoration. It's a routing layer tuned by the outcomes of your organization's own runs — which strategies produced work that passed verification, and which didn't.
