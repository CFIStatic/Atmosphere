Atmosphere text messaging

Atmosphere (operated by Jettx LLC) sends a small number of account text messages (SMS). This page explains who gets them, how people opt in, and how to stop.

Who gets texts

  • Crew members invited by their employer. A contractor that uses Atmosphere can invite a crew member to its workspace by mobile number. The crew member gives that number to their employer and agrees to get the invite text.
  • People signing in. Anyone signing in to Atmosphere can choose to get a one-time sign-in code by text at the number they type in themselves.

How people opt in

1. Team invite form (employer invites a crew member)

The crew member gives their own mobile number to their employer and agrees, in person or verbally, to get a text from Atmosphere. The employer's admin enters the number in Atmosphere at platform.atmosphereteam.com under Settings > Organization > Invite teammates > Phone (or on a job's Access tab when inviting crew by mobile number). Before the invite can be sent, the admin must check this required box:

This person agreed to get a text from Atmosphere with their invite. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

The invite button stays disabled until the box is checked, and our server rejects any phone invite sent without this confirmation.

2. Sign-in page (person requests a code)

On the Atmosphere sign-in page (app.atmosphereteam.com, the Field Capture app, and platform.atmosphereteam.com/login), a person taps Text me a code instead, enters their own mobile number, and taps Text me a code. Right next to the button, the page states:

We'll text you a 6-digit code. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Message types

  • Invite text. One text with a link to sign in to the employer's Atmosphere workspace.
  • Sign-in codes. A one-time 6-digit code, sent only when the person asks for one.

Atmosphere does not send marketing or promotional texts.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. The invite is sent once per invitation; sign-in codes are sent only on request.

Msg & data rates may apply.

Sample messages

Jack Cyganiak invited you to join Jettx LLC on Atmosphere. Open https://app.atmosphereteam.com/login?invited=1 and sign in with this number. We will text you a 6-digit code. Reply STOP to opt out.
Your Atmosphere sign-in code is 482913. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Reply STOP to any Atmosphere text and we will stop texting that number. Reply START to resubscribe. For help, reply HELP or email hello@atmosphereteam.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and text-messaging consent are used only to send the messages described above. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms.
