Legal

Legal holds, preservation and takedown requests

One address for every legal request about footage and records stored in Atmosphere: legal@atmosphereteam.com

Atmosphere stores job-site video, photos, transcripts and the records built from them on behalf of the contractors who use it. Homeowners, customers, attorneys, insurers and government authorities sometimes need that material kept, locked down, or removed. This page explains how to ask and what happens next.

What you can request

  • Legal hold or preservation. Keep specific footage and records exactly as they are, so nothing is deleted, purged or edited while a claim, dispute, investigation or lawsuit is pending.
  • Restricted access. Limit who can view or share specific footage, for example while a matter is open or because it captures something sensitive.
  • Takedown or deletion. Remove footage that you believe was recorded or shared improperly, or exercise a privacy right over your personal data.
  • Disclosure or production. Obtain copies of records, for example under a subpoena, court order or warrant.

How to send a request

Email legal@atmosphereteam.com. Every message is logged and reviewed by a person. Please include as much of the following as you can:

  • The job: the property address, job or claim number, and the contractor's company name if you know it.
  • Dates: the date or date range of the work or footage in question.
  • Parties: who you are, who you represent, and the other people or companies involved.
  • Case details: the court, case or matter number, claim number, or agency reference.
  • What you want: hold, restrict access, take down, or produce, and which footage or records it covers.
  • The legal document: attach any preservation letter, subpoena, court order, warrant or other legal process.
  • How to reach you: a name, phone number and return email address.

If you use Atmosphere yourself, you can also contact your company's administrator, who can place a hold on a job from inside the app.

Preservation versus disclosure

We treat these differently on purpose.

  • Preservation is honored promptly. When we receive a reasonable, specific request to preserve records, we place them on legal hold. Held records are kept intact and are excluded from normal retention, deletion and purge schedules, including deletion by the account holder, until the hold is released.
  • Disclosure requires valid legal process. We do not hand over customer footage or records to third parties because of an email alone. Production to anyone other than the customer who owns the records requires a valid subpoena, court order, warrant or other legal process, or the customer's authorization, unless the law requires otherwise.
  • We may notify the affected customer of a request for their records unless we are legally prohibited from doing so or notice would create a risk of harm.

How we handle timing

  • Every request is reviewed by a person. How long it takes depends on the request and the legal process involved.
  • Preservation: we can place a legal hold on clearly identified records while a request is reviewed or formal process is obtained.
  • Emergencies: requests involving an imminent risk of death or serious physical injury receive expedited review. Put "EMERGENCY" in the subject line.

Law enforcement and government authorities

Authorities should send requests from an official government email address to legal@atmosphereteam.com, with the legal process attached. Preservation requests are honored while you obtain formal process.

Other questions

For general support, email hello@atmosphereteam.com. See also our Privacy policy and Terms of service.

Sending a request to this address does not create an attorney-client relationship and does not waive any rights or objections of Jettx LLC or its customers.
