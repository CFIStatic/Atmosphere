Careers

Engineering and sales for Work Verification

Atmosphere builds one system for service contractors: Field Capture on site, and the Evidence Platform for the record that follows. We are hiring software engineers and sales professionals to take that product to market and keep it reliable in production.

THE WORK OPEN ROLES FIRST 90 DAYS PROCESS FAQ APPLY
The work

A production product, sold to operators

Software that holds the record

Engineers own Field Capture, the Evidence Platform, and the APIs behind them — TypeScript, React, Node.js, and Postgres in production, with tests around the paths that cannot fail quietly.

Sales to the people who run the work

Sales speaks with contractor owners and operators. The product is one subscription: crews film the day; the office, reviewers, and counterparties work from the same verified record.

The job site is the source of truth

Both roles stay close to how work is actually performed. Product decisions and sales conversations start from real jobs, not a generic field-service story.

Open roles

Software engineering and sales

Full-time roles. Expand a listing for responsibilities and the experience we need; apply with the form at the bottom of this page.

Software Engineer FULL-TIME

Build and maintain Atmosphere's Work Verification product. That includes the Field Capture experience, the Evidence Platform, organization setup, and the Express APIs that sit in front of Postgres. You will take work from the interface through the server and into the database, with automated tests on the paths that protect the record.

We need production TypeScript and React, comfort with Node.js services and relational data, and the judgment to ship carefully. Experience with video, mobile capture, or B2B operations software is useful. Contractor-industry background is not required; willingness to learn how a job is filmed, verified, and shared is.

Apply for this role →
Account Executive FULL-TIME

Own the sales cycle for Atmosphere with service contractors — restoration, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and related trades. You will identify the decision-maker, demonstrate Work Verification on the kind of job they run, and take an organization from first conversation through a live workspace.

We need demonstrated B2B sales, preferably to contractors or other field-service operators. You should be able to explain Field Capture and the Evidence Platform in the customer's language, keep an accurate pipeline, and follow a deal through onboarding. Technical training is part of the role; product fluency is expected.

Apply for this role →
Business Development Representative FULL-TIME

Build and qualify the pipeline for Account Executives. You will research contractor firms, open conversations with owners and operators, and book product demonstrations that are worth the calendar — not generic software pitches.

We need disciplined outreach, clear written and spoken communication, and comfort talking with people who run crews. Prior SDR or BDR experience in B2B software, construction, or field services is a strong fit. You will learn the verification workflow in enough detail to answer first questions before handing the conversation to sales.

Apply for this role →

First 90 days

Clear ownership, in product or in pipeline

WEEK 1

Context, then contribution

Access, product walkthrough, and a first assignment — a production change for engineering, or a defined territory and target list for sales.

MONTH 1

Own a named area

An engineer owns a surface or service. A sales hire owns a set of accounts and a repeatable demonstration of Work Verification.

Measured against the work

Hiring process

Four steps

A short process built around the work of the role, not abstract puzzles.

01

Introduction

A conversation with the team you would join. We cover the product, the role, and your relevant work.

02

Role exercise

Engineering: a scoped task against the product. Sales: a walkthrough of how you would take a contractor from first contact to a demonstration.

03

Team conversation

Meet the people you would work with. Review the product, the customers, and how decisions are made.

04

Decision

A clear answer, with reasons, typically within a week of the team conversation.

Questions

Asked in most first conversations

Do I need contractor or field-service experience?

For sales, experience selling to contractors or other field-service operators is a strong advantage. For engineering, it is not required. Both roles need a willingness to learn how a job is filmed, verified, and shared.

Are these roles remote?

These are full-time roles. Work location is confirmed during the process so we can be specific rather than generic on this page.

How long does the process take?

Typically about two weeks from the first conversation to a decision. If we are slower than that, you should follow up.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Yes. Submit one application, name the roles in the message, and we will route it.
