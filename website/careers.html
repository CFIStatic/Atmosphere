Build the proof machine

We're a small team building serious infrastructure for an industry that shows up when things go wrong. The work is agents, evidence, and trust — for people who hold moisture meters, not just laptops.

How we work

Small team, real jobs, everything on the record

Remote-first, US hours

We work where we live and meet where the work is. Writing beats meetings; records beat memory.

Ride-alongs are part of the job

Everyone — engineers included — spends time on real job sites with real contractors. You can't automate work you've never watched.

Proof over promises, internally too

The principles we sell are the ones we run on. Demos are replays of real runs, and "done" means verified.
