Candidate questions

Asked in almost every intro call

Do I need restoration or construction experience?

For the Estimating Lead — yes, that's the job. For everything else: no, but you need to want it. The ride-alongs exist so the industry stops being an abstraction by your first quarter.

How remote is "remote"?

Fully — we hire across US time zones and run on written records instead of standing meetings. We get together in person roughly quarterly, plus the job site visits.

How long does the process take?

About two weeks from intro call to decision, and the work sample is paid. If we're slower than that, you're allowed to nudge us.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Yes — send one application and say so in the message. Range is a feature on a team this small.

Tell us which role brought you here — or make the case for one we haven't listed.
