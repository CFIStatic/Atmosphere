How we work

Small team, real jobs, everything on the record

In person, every day

We build in one room. Decisions happen at the desk beside you, not in a thread three time zones away.

Ride-alongs are part of the job

Everyone — engineers included — spends time on real job sites with real contractors. You can't automate work you've never watched.

Proof over promises, internally too

The principles we sell are the ones we run on. Demos are replays of real runs, and "done" means verified.

What you get

Paid, covered, equipped — and heard

The package is built the way the product is: nothing clever, nothing missing.

Comp

Paid like a senior hire

Salary that matches the experience the work actually demands — no discount for the privilege of an early seat.

Health

Health, dental, vision

Fully covered for you, heavily subsidized for your family — from day one, not day ninety.

Kit

Hardware, no argument

The machine you want, the desk you want, and the second monitor — asked for once, not justified twice.

Travel

Ride-alongs, paid

Time on real job sites is part of the job, so the flights, the truck, and the steel toes are on us.

Time

Time off people actually take

Flexible PTO with a floor, not a ceiling — the thing we track is that you took enough.

Seat

A real seat at a small table

Early team means you shape what Atmosphere becomes — the roadmap argument is yours to win.

Candidate questions

Asked in almost every intro call

Do I need field service or contractor experience?

For the Scope Lead — yes, that's the job. For everything else: no, but you need to want it. The ride-alongs exist so the industry stops being an abstraction by your first quarter.

Is there a remote option?

No — these roles are on-site. A team this small moves faster in one room, and the ride-alongs only work when you can get in the truck with us. We help with relocation for the right person.

How long does the process take?

About two weeks from intro call to decision, and the work sample is paid. If we're slower than that, you're allowed to nudge us.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Yes — send one application and say so in the message. Range is a feature on a team this small.
