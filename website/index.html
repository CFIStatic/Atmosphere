How it works

From walk-through to done.

Three steps. The crew only does the first one.

01

Walk the job

Start recording in Field Capture and do the walk-through. Video and audio upload on their own, and pick up again when signal comes back.

02

Atmosphere builds the job file

Transcript with speaker names, rooms, key moments, your uploaded documents and a timeline. Nobody types notes.

03

Ask, then act

Ask anything about the job and get the answer with the quote and timestamp. Hand the follow-up to Computer, and approve before anything is sent, submitted or paid.

Ask

Ask the job anything.

Answers come from what was actually said and filmed, with the exact quote and the moment to play it. If it isn't in the file, Atmosphere says so.

  • Reads the scope, estimate or policy PDFs you upload
  • Knows who said what
  • Searches the web for code or manufacturer questions
  • Drafts the homeowner update or crew invite
