The main product

Construction runs on word against word. This ends it.

Disputed Tuesdays, unpayable pay apps, jobs read back eighteen months later from a photo folder — all the same failure: no record. The Work Verification Platform makes the record, and it costs the crew two presses of one button.

01

One button, all day

Tap it on arrival, hold it when the day is done. One recording, every job in it, nothing to tag.

02

Checked at the door

GPS on the site, clock on the day, hash sealed at arrival. A clip filmed two miles away is flagged before anybody watches it.

03

Read by the assistant

AI reads the footage against the agreed scope and writes the narrated report — coverage computed, never claimed.

04

Held and handed over

An append-only chain of custody, shared account-to-account with the adjuster who releases the money.

How a run works

Hand it work the way you'd hand it to a teammate

Four stages, every time. The last two are the reason contractors trust the first two.

01

You hand it work

Build this estimate. Enter this into the portal. Pull these numbers. Describe the task in plain language — the way it's actually assigned.

02

The agent executes

Atmosphere signs in and does the work — in your systems, in the carrier's, or on a real machine. Every step is recorded as it happens.

03

The Verifier checks

A second agent goes back and looks, in read-only. It confirms the work against the original task and flags anything it isn't sure of.

04

You hold the record

The run lands in the audit trail. Replay it step by step, or read it back like a receipt.

Security
