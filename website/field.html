What it does

Three tools, zero typing

Nobody documents a job site with their thumbs. Field Capture films the work the way crews already move through it — two short videos a day, anchor the address, walk what changed.

Record

Audio and video, from the walk-through

Start recording and do the job. The walk-through, the readings you call out, the customer's answers — captured once, attached to the job, never retyped.

CapturesAudio · video, hands-free
AttachesTo the job, as evidence
Speak

A conversation, not a form

Hold a spoken conversation with an assistant while you work: log a note, ask what the scope requires for this step, get reminded what you haven't filmed yet.

HearsYour voice, over the fans
AnswersIn the middle of the work, out loud
See

The camera names what it sees

Point the phone and the app identifies what's in frame — materials, damage, equipment — so the record carries names and counts, not just footage someone has to scrub through later.

SeesMaterials · damage · equipment
WritesNames and counts into the record
Where it goes

Capture in, verification out

What Field Capture films is what the Evidence Platform holds — clips checked at the door for location and time, read against the agreed scope, and stored with a chain of custody behind them. Better capture on Tuesday is a settled question eighteen months from now.
