The estimators

How the Mitigation and Construction Estimators read evidence and write Xactimate estimates.

Mitigation Estimator

Give it what the job actually produced and it writes the estimate:

  • Reads: the DocuSketch scan, the MICA drying report, iPhone photos, and field notes.
  • Classifies: the loss against IICRC S500.
  • Writes: a priced, documented estimate for Xactimate, in the carrier's program terms.

Then it does the pass estimators rarely have time for: it reads the record again looking for work that was performed but never billed, and adds the line items with the evidence attached.

Construction Estimator

The rebuild estimate starts where mitigation ended. The agent signs in to DocuSketch itself, reads the scan and the field photos, identifies the matching job in your CRM, and reads the mitigation estimate that came before it. What comes out is the construction estimate for Xactimate — grounded in what was demoed, dried, and documented.

Your pricing assumptions

Per-organization settings hold your margin, overhead & profit, tax, and cost-basis assumptions. The estimators price from them, and the Manager Platform's job costing reads the same numbers — so the bid and the books are never two different opinions.

Every estimate is a run

Estimates are written as immutable snapshots, and the run that produced one lands in the audit trail: what the agent read, what it wrote, and what the Verifier confirmed. If a carrier questions a line, you replay the run that priced it.
