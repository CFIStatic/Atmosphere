Work Verification company

We build the record before the argument starts

Atmosphere is a Work Verification company for contractors and field workers. Two parts of one system: Field Capture films each day of work on site; the Evidence Platform checks the footage at the door, reads it against the agreed scope, and holds it in a chain of custody both sides can hand to whoever releases the money. One subscription covers both.

Construction and restoration run on trust — and trust breaks the same way every time. A disputed Tuesday eight months ago. A pay application nobody can verify without a site visit. An adjuster handed a photo folder and asked to settle from it. Different trades, same failure underneath: two companies working from different facts, and no record of either.

We did not set out to build another operations platform or another estimating tool. We set out to end the word-against-word problem — to make the proof before the phone call, so when someone asks what happened on site, the answer is footage with a location, a timestamp, a hash, and a narrated reading against the scope both sides already agreed to.

That is Work Verification: two short videos a day, a shot list built from the job's own scope, integrity checks that run before anyone watches the clip, and an append-only chain of custody that survives eighteen months and a mediator. Field Capture is how crews film on site; the Evidence Platform is where capture becomes evidence.

Restoration came first because it is the hardest version of the problem — a carrier, a homeowner, and a contractor who all need proof of the same work. Roofers, general contractors, and specialty trades face the same dispute with less paperwork. The product is the same: film it, check it, read it, hold it, share it account-to-account with the person who needs to see it.

The Evidence Platform in depth →
Principles

What makes footage evidence

Filmed on site, on the day

Location and time are checked before meaning is read. A clip from two miles away never passes as today's work.

Unchecked is not clean

Unknown reads as unverified — not pass, not fail. The system must be able to say "we could not tell."

AI reads, people decide

The assistant's reading and a person's acceptance are different claims. Money moves on the second, never silently on the first.

The log outranks its owner

Custody is append-only. Nothing edits history — not the GC, not the sub, not us.

Both sides, same file

Verification is not surveillance. The sub's proof and the GC's certainty are one record, shared account-to-account.
