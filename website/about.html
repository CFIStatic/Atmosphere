Company

Restoration is trust work

A home at its worst moment. A carrier that needs everything documented. A contractor in the middle, buried in paperwork nobody became a builder to do. That's the job Atmosphere took.

Mitigation moves fast because it has to — water doesn't wait for data entry. The documentation debt piles up behind the work: scans to read, photos to file, losses to classify, estimates to write to someone else's program terms, portals to feed. The industry's open secret is that good work goes unbilled simply because nobody had time to write it down.

AI can do that paperwork now. But handing your claims to an AI only works if you can check what it did — trust in this industry is built on documentation, and an AI that can't show its work doesn't deserve yours. So we built Atmosphere proof-first: a second agent verifies every run in a browser that can't change anything, and everything every agent does lands in an append-only audit trail your organization can replay step by step.

And we made a bet about where the work happens: we go to it. Atmosphere signs in to the carrier portal, reads the DocuSketch scan, operates the machine running Xactimate. Your systems stay your systems. The work gets done where it already lives — and the proof comes back with it.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to

Proof over promises

Every run ends in a record you can replay. If we can't show it, we don't claim it.

Go to the work

We sign in where the work lives. We don't ask your data to migrate before the AI can help.

A second set of eyes

No agent grades its own work. The Verifier checks in read-only, and asks when it's unsure.

Write it down

The record is append-only. Nothing edits history — not an agent, not an admin, not us.

Store nothing we can't protect

No plaintext passwords, no tokens in the page, and a database that enforces who sees what.
