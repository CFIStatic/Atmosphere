Privacy policy

Last updated July 31, 2026. Written to be read, like everything else here.

What we hold

Your account carries an email address and a display name. Your organization carries the working data your team puts into it — customers, properties, jobs, estimates, captures from the field, and the runs agents perform. That data belongs to your organization and is scoped to it: Row-Level Security at the database means another organization's members and agents cannot read it.

What we never hold

  • Your password. It goes to our authentication provider, which stores only a cryptographic hash. We never see or store the plaintext.
  • Your card number. Payments are processed by Stripe; we hold references and receipts, not card details.
  • Session tokens in the page. Sign-in tokens live in httpOnly cookies that page JavaScript cannot read.

Forms on this site

The contact and careers forms email what you submit to our inbox so a person can reply. We don't build marketing profiles from them, and we don't sell or rent anything you send us — or anything else.

Who processes data on our behalf

Atmosphere runs on a small set of processors: Supabase (authentication and database), Stripe (payments), the model providers that power agent runs, and an email provider for transactional mail. Each receives only what its job requires.

Agents and your other systems

When you connect an outside site or a computer, agents act only under the access your organization granted, and every step is recorded in your audit trail — which is append-only, so the record of what was done with your data cannot be quietly rewritten.

The learning layer

Runs are scored to improve how work is routed for your organization. Your data stays inside your organization's boundary while that happens.

Retention and deletion

Working data lives as long as your organization does. Write to us to ask for an export or deletion of your account data; audit records are append-only by design and are removed when the organization that owns them is deleted.

Questions

Use the contact page. A person reads it.

This policy is a plain-language draft pending review by counsel; the practices it describes reflect how the product is actually built.
