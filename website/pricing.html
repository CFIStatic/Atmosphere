Usage

Three layers — one invoice you can read aloud

Customers see work performed, not infrastructure. Atmosphere tracks tokens and provider costs internally for our own accounting; your bill is always platform access + jobs + exceptional compute.

01

Base platform fee — $599/mo

Fixed monthly subscription for Field Capture and the Evidence Platform. Always the first line on your invoice. Negotiable for enterprise.

02

Job usage — 50 included, then $30/job

Each unique job Atmosphere actively processes in a billing period counts once — ten AI actions on the same job still count as one job. Jobs 1–50 are included; job 51 and above are $30 each.

03

Exceptional compute — 1,000 units included

Long-form video, large document sets, and unusually expensive autonomous workflows draw from included Compute Units. Only the overage appears on your bill — never raw token counts.

Job usage

Counted once per job, per period

A job becomes billable when Atmosphere runs a qualifying workflow against it — daily work verification, video analysis, document processing, or another configured workflow type. Ten AI calls on the same job still count as one job.

Overage math

62 − 50 = 12 billable overage jobs × $30 = $360. The platform fee ($599) is separate and always charged. Multiple verification runs on the same job in the same month still count as one job.

Exceptional compute

Heavy workloads, understandable units

When verification requires unusually expensive processing — hours of video, multimodal analysis, large commercial jobs — usage draws from your 1,000 included Compute Units each period. You see compute overage on your bill, not millions of tokens.

Video verification hours

Long-form or multimodal video work is tracked as verification hours on your usage dashboard — a number you can explain to a customer or CFO.

Compute overage

When included Compute Units are exhausted, overage is billed at your plan's rate. The conversion between internal AI cost and Compute Units is configurable and snapshotted when a billing period closes.

Provider independence

Atmosphere can switch models and providers without changing your pricing model. As inference costs change, your invoice stays based on work performed.

What is never on your invoice

We track these internally for cost accounting. You never see them as customer-facing line items:

  • LLM tokens — input, output, cached, or reasoning tokens
  • Model or provider names — Claude, GPT, Gemini, etc.
  • Per-API-call micro-charges — one job can involve dozens of AI steps; you pay for the job once
  • Separate Field Capture or Evidence Platform fees — both are included in the $599 platform fee

During migration, some legacy agent paths may still draw from prepaid credits in the app. New work bills through jobs + compute above. See Billing & usage docs for the full model.

Questions

The ones we actually get asked

Where do I see my current usage?

Sign in and open your usage dashboard. It shows jobs processed this period, video verification hours, compute units used, and your estimated bill — updated as work runs. You must be signed in; the dashboard is not public.

What is included in the subscription?

One monthly fee covers the full Work Verification system: Field Capture for filming and checking work on site, and the Evidence Platform for recording, verifying, storing, and sharing that work. Both are required — they ship together.

What does the monthly platform fee cover?

Access to the complete Work Verification system — Field Capture on site, the Verifier, the audit trail, durable storage, and sharing. Usage for jobs and exceptional compute is billed separately.

How is usage billed?

Three lines: $599 platform fee, then $30 for each job above 50 processed that month, then exceptional compute overage if you exceed 1,000 included Compute Units. You see jobs and compute units — not token counts or model names.

What counts as a processed job?

A unique job where Atmosphere runs a qualifying workflow during the billing period. The job is counted once regardless of how many AI actions occur inside it.

What are Field Capture and the Evidence Platform?

They are the two parts of one system, not separate purchases. Field Capture is how crews film daily work on site. The Evidence Platform is where that footage is checked, read against the scope, and held in a chain of custody. You get both on every subscription.

Can our plan be customized?

Yes. Base fees, included jobs, per-job rates, compute allowances, and enterprise terms are all configurable. Negotiated overrides are stored per organization.

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

The learning layer scores your organization's own runs and improves how work is routed for you. Your data stays inside your organization, behind Row-Level Security — another org's agents cannot see it, at the database layer.

Put it on a real job

Not a sandbox — your job, your scope, your customer. One subscription, the full system.
