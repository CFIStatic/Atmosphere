Questions

The ones we actually get asked

Where do I see my current usage?

Sign in and open your usage dashboard or Settings → Billing. It shows seats on the plan, usage billed the day it ran, and your estimated bill. You must be signed in; the dashboard is not public.

Do homeowners pay for Atmosphere?

No. Homeowners (and other job-progress invitees) create a free account with the same email as the invite and a password. They only see invited jobs — no Stripe checkout, no plan, no trial, and no Field Capture seat.

What is included in the subscription?

Every self-serve plan covers Field Capture and the Evidence Platform. Starter is $399/mo with 1 Field Capture account, Work Verification is $849/mo with 3, and Scale is $1,999/mo with 10. Both software parts ship together.

How many Field Capture accounts are included?

1 on Starter, 3 on Work Verification, 10 on Scale. Extra accounts are $125/month each. Office-only Global Admins do not use a seat unless they also use Field Capture. Evidence Platform access is not a separate seat.

How is usage billed?

Usage (AI and token analysis) is billed the day it is used. You see a usage line on the invoice — not token counts, model names, or a published per-job dollar rate.

What is the Field Capture Chest Mount?

An optional one-time hardware add-on at $99.99. It ships in 2–5 days. Field Capture software does not require the mount. See the kit.

What are Field Capture and the Evidence Platform?

They are the two parts of one system, not separate purchases. Field Capture is how crews film daily work on site. The Evidence Platform is where that footage is checked, read against the scope, and held in a chain of custody. You get both on every subscription.

Can our plan be customized?

Self-serve plans are Starter, Work Verification, and Scale. For more seats or custom terms, contact us for Enterprise.

Do prices increase over time?

Yes. Monthly plan fees, extra Field Capture seats, and usage rates increase by 10% each year on the anniversary of your paid plan start, with 30 days advance notice by email. The one-time Chest Mount hardware purchase is excluded. See the Terms of Use (Fees and Modifications).

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

Production AI vendors we use are configured not to train on customer data. We do not use your organization's job or account data to train shared models available to other customers. Within your organization, features may score your own runs to improve how work is routed for you, behind org isolation (including Row-Level Security where used).
