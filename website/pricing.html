Questions

The ones we actually get asked

What does the $199.99 seat include?

Platform access for that user — not usage. Load prepaid credits before agents run.

How is usage priced?

Five times what Atmosphere pays per million tokens for each model. The table above is the full sell-side rate card.

What happens when the balance hits zero?

New runs pause. Auto-reload or a manual top-up resumes work; nothing already in your record is lost.

Do I have to buy all four platforms?

No. Take the product you need today. They share one record, so adding another later has nothing to import.

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

The learning layer scores your organization’s own runs and improves routing for you. Your data stays inside your organization, behind Row-Level Security.
