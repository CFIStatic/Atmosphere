Provider independence

Atmosphere can switch models and providers without changing the published subscription or seat prices.

What is never on your invoice

We track these internally for cost accounting. You never see them as customer-facing line items:

  • LLM tokens — input, output, cached, or reasoning tokens
  • Model or provider names — Claude, GPT, Gemini, etc.
  • Per-job dollar rates — usage is billed the day analysis runs, not as a published per-job fee
  • Separate Field Capture or Evidence Platform fees — both are included in the $599 platform fee
  • Job-scoped invited workers — they do not consume a Field Capture seat

During migration, some legacy agent paths may still draw from prepaid credits in the app. That ledger is legacy — new work bills through seats + same-day usage. See Billing & usage docs for the full model.

Questions

The ones we actually get asked

Where do I see my current usage?

Sign in and open your usage dashboard or Settings → Billing. It shows seats on the plan, usage billed the day it ran, and your estimated bill. You must be signed in; the dashboard is not public.

What is included in the subscription?

One monthly fee of $599 covers the full Work Verification system: Field Capture for filming and checking work on site, and the Evidence Platform for recording, verifying, storing, and sharing that work. The subscription includes 3 Field Capture accounts. Both software parts are required — they ship together.

How many Field Capture accounts are included?

Three. Additional Field Capture accounts are $100/month each. The Evidence Platform is included with the subscription — it is not a separate seat.

How is usage billed?

Usage (AI and token analysis) is billed the day it is used. You see a usage line on the invoice — not token counts, model names, or a published per-job dollar rate.

What is the Field Capture Chest Mount?

An optional one-time hardware add-on at $49.99. It ships in 2–5 days. Field Capture software does not require the mount. See the kit.

What are Field Capture and the Evidence Platform?

They are the two parts of one system, not separate purchases. Field Capture is how crews film daily work on site. The Evidence Platform is where that footage is checked, read against the scope, and held in a chain of custody. You get both on every subscription.

Can our plan be customized?

Yes. Seat counts and enterprise terms are configurable. Negotiated overrides are stored per organization.

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

The learning layer scores your organization's own runs and improves how work is routed for you. Your data stays inside your organization, behind Row-Level Security — another org's agents cannot see it, at the database layer.

Put it on a real job

Not a sandbox — your job, your scope, your customer. One subscription, the full system.
