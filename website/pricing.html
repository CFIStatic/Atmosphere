Provider independence

What is never on your invoice

We track these internally for cost accounting. You never see them as customer-facing line items:

  • Model or provider names — Claude, GPT, Gemini, etc. on the marketing invoice
  • A leftover seat / credit catalog — not this product
  • Separate Field Capture or Evidence Platform fees — both are included in the $599 platform fee
  • Job-scoped invited workers — they do not consume a Field Capture seat

A leftover seat / credit catalog still has API handlers so existing Stripe events do not break. It is not this product. See Billing & usage docs for the full model.

Questions

The ones we actually get asked

Where do I see my current usage?

Sign in and open your usage dashboard. It shows jobs processed this period, video verification hours, compute units used, and your estimated bill — updated as work runs. You must be signed in; the dashboard is not public.

What is included in the subscription?

$599/month covers Work Verification: Field Capture on site and the Evidence Platform for the record. That includes 3 Field Capture accounts. Extra accounts are $100/month. Optional Chest Mount hardware is $49.99.

What does the monthly platform fee cover?

Access to the complete Work Verification system — Field Capture on site, the Verifier, the audit trail, durable storage, and sharing — including 3 Field Capture accounts. Extra seats and same-day AI usage are billed separately.

How is usage billed?

$599 platform fee (3 Field Capture accounts included), $100 per extra Field Capture account, and AI usage billed the day it is used. Receipts land in Settings → Billing.

How many Field Capture accounts are included?

Three. The 4th Field Capture account needs an extra seat at $100/month. Add seats from Settings → Billing or when an invite is blocked.

What are Field Capture and the Evidence Platform?

They are the two parts of one system, not separate purchases. Field Capture is how crews film daily work on site. The Evidence Platform is where that footage is checked, read against the scope, and held in a chain of custody. You get both on every subscription.

Can our plan be customized?

Yes. Enterprise terms can be arranged with sales. The published catalog is Work Verification, extra Field Capture seats, same-day usage, and the Chest Mount.

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

The learning layer scores your organization's own runs and improves how work is routed for you. Your data stays inside your organization, behind Row-Level Security — another org's agents cannot see it, at the database layer.

Put it on a real job

Not a sandbox — your job, your scope, your customer. One subscription, the full system.
