Questions

The ones we actually get asked

What counts as a user?

Anyone linked to your organization with the join code — office staff on the dashboard and field technicians on the technician app alike. You can see every linked account from the dashboard.

Do you mark up model usage?

No. Atmosphere connects your own Anthropic API key, and Anthropic bills you directly for what the agents use. Our plans cover the platform only.

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

The learning layer scores your organization's own runs and improves how work is routed for you. Your data stays inside your organization, behind Row-Level Security — another org's agents can't see it, at the database layer.

Can we change plans?

Yes — plans are monthly, and you can move between Field and Office as your team changes. Organization agreements are annual and shaped with you.

Not sure which fits?

Tell us how your jobs run and we'll tell you honestly — including if the answer is "start smaller."
