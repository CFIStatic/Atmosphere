Every plan includes the audit trail and the Verifier — proof is never an upgrade. The seat price buys access and throughput; token usage is billed separately on every plan, at the rates below. Annual billing is shown as the equivalent monthly rate.

Usage

You pay for tokens, and only for tokens

There is no bundled allowance to run out of and none to waste. Agents spend tokens while they work, you are billed for exactly those, and a quiet week costs you the seat and nothing more.

2×

Twice the model's list price

One number, published below, the same on every plan and every platform. What we add on top pays for running the agents, the Verifier's second pass, and the record.

metered

Counted by the provider, not by us

Token counts come from the model provider's own response, never from the browser — so what you are billed is what was actually spent.

ledger

Every charge is on the record

Usage lands in an append-only ledger you can read by run, by agent, and by platform — the same discipline as the audit trail.

Questions

The ones we actually get asked

What happens after the 30 days?

You pick a plan and keep going, or you stop — nothing auto-upgrades in the dark. The work you did during the trial stays in your audit trail either way.

Is the trial limited?

Not in features. You get the whole platform for thirty days, and usage is billed at the same rate card as everyone else — so what you spend testing is what it would actually cost you.

Why twice the list price?

Because a token is not the product. The margin covers running the agents, the Verifier's second pass over the work, and keeping the record — none of which the model provider does for you.

Do I have to buy all four platforms?

No. They are four products — take the one that hurts most today. They read the same record, so adding another later has nothing to import.

What do the throughput multipliers mean?

They are ceilings on how much work you can push through at once, relative to Pro. Max 5× and Max 20× exist for people running agents continuously rather than a few times a day.

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

The learning layer scores your organization's own runs and improves how work is routed for you. Your data stays inside your organization, behind Row-Level Security — another org's agents cannot see it, at the database layer.

Take thirty days on a real job

Not a sandbox — your scan, your claim, your carrier. That is the only test that tells you anything.
