Talk to us

Every plan includes the audit trail and the Verifier — proof is never an upgrade. The seat price buys access and throughput; token usage is billed separately on every plan, at the flat rate below. Annual billing is shown as the equivalent monthly rate.

Usage

You pay for tokens, and only for tokens

No bundled allowance, no postpaid tab. You load credits, agents draw them down as they work, and a quiet week costs you the seat and nothing more — unused credits just wait.

prepaid

Credits first, tokens second

You buy usage credits before agents spend them. One credit is one dollar of usage at the flat rate — so there is never a surprise bill at month end, on either side of the table.

metered

Counted by the provider, not by us

Every token drawn from your balance is counted from the model provider's own response, never from the browser — and lands in an append-only ledger you can read by run, agent, and platform.

zero

At zero, work pauses — never runs a tab

An empty balance pauses new runs rather than billing past it. Auto-reload tops you up before that ever happens, or reload manually if you'd rather approve every dollar.

What it covers

Everything behind a run — the frontier models doing the work, the Verifier's second pass, and the record you can replay. These two numbers are the whole price list.

You can estimate the bill yourself

Two numbers and the token counts in your audit trail. No model mix to work out, no per-agent table to reconcile at month end.

You always get our best

The rate is the same whichever model does the work, so every job simply goes to whatever handles it best. There is never a reason for your work to get anything less.

Better routing shows up as better work

The learning layer improves which model handles what, run over run. On a flat rate that lands in the estimates you send, not in a fluctuating invoice.

Auto-reload

Set a threshold and an amount, and when the balance dips below the line a reload buys itself — a Friday-afternoon estimate never stops halfway. Defaults: reload $25 when the balance falls under $5.

Manual reload

Leave auto-reload off and every dollar is one you approved. Work pauses at zero and resumes the moment you top up — nothing in your record is lost while paused.

A spend cap, either way

Set a monthly spend limit on the organization and no reload — automatic or manual — will carry usage past it.

Questions

The ones we actually get asked

What happens after the 30 days?

You pick a plan and keep going, or you stop — nothing auto-upgrades in the dark. The work you did during the trial stays in your audit trail either way.

Is the trial limited?

Not in features. You get the whole platform for thirty days, and usage is billed at the same flat rate as everyone else — so what you spend testing is what it would actually cost you.

Why one flat rate?

So the math stays simple: two numbers and the token counts in your audit trail. And because a token is not the product — the rate covers the agents doing the work, the Verifier's second pass over it, and the record you can replay. None of that comes from a model provider.

Do I have to buy all four platforms?

No. They are four products — take the one that hurts most today. They read the same record, so adding another later has nothing to import.

What happens when the balance hits zero?

New runs pause — Atmosphere never spends money you haven't loaded. Turn on auto-reload and the balance tops itself up before that point; keep it manual and one click resumes work. Either way, nothing already in your record is lost.

Where do I choose auto or manual reload?

Settings → Billing. Auto-reload takes a threshold and an amount; leaving it off means every purchase is one you made yourself. You can also set a monthly spend cap that neither mode will cross.

What do the throughput multipliers mean?

They are ceilings on how much work you can push through at once, relative to Pro. Max 5× and Max 20× exist for people running agents continuously rather than a few times a day.

Does Atmosphere train on our data?

The learning layer scores your organization's own runs and improves how work is routed for you. Your data stays inside your organization, behind Row-Level Security — another org's agents cannot see it, at the database layer.

Take thirty days on a real job

Not a sandbox — your scan, your claim, your carrier. That is the only test that tells you anything.
