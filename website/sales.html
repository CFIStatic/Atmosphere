Sales Platform

Win the work

Leads come in, get worked, turn into bids, and are won or lost. The Sales Platform models that job as data — then puts an agent on top of it that drafts, follows up, and notices the deal going quiet before you do.

PIPELINE THE AGENT HANDOFF
The workspace

The pipeline, modelled properly

Before an agent can help, the work has to exist as something other than a notebook and a memory. Leads, bids, and outcomes are first-class records — org-scoped, and visible to your team only.

Leads

Every lead in one place

A lead carries the property, the damage or the scope — a hail-hit roof, a burst pipe, a remodel — who referred it, and everything said since. No more reconstructing a week of history from a phone log before you can call someone back.

HoldsProperty · contact · source · full history
MovesNew → working → bid → won or lost
ScopesTo your organization — RLS at the database
Bids

Bids that come from the inspection, not the memory

A bid is built from what was actually captured on the walk-through — the notes, the photos, the measurements — and stays attached to the lead it came from.

ReadsInspection notes · photos · measurements
WritesA priced bid, attached to the lead
KeepsEvery version — what changed, and when
The agent

Never bored, never behind

The agent works on the same records a person does — no parallel system, no export. It drafts the tedious parts and watches the parts that go quiet.

Drafts

The writing nobody has time for

Estimates from inspection notes. The follow-up you meant to send Tuesday. A summary of everything that has happened on a lead, for the person picking it up cold.

DraftsEstimates · follow-ups · lead summaries
WaitsYou approve before anything is sent
Watches

Pipeline that has gone cold

Deals do not die loudly. They just stop — and nobody notices, because a quiet lead generates no phone calls. The agent counts the days and says so.

FlagsLeads with no movement
SurfacesWhat is owed, and to whom
RecordsEvery action — in the audit trail
The handoff

Sold work arrives already staffed with facts

Sales stops at won. Everything the rep learned — the scope, the customer, the promises made — crosses into the Operations Platform as the job's starting record, so the project manager is not re-interviewing the customer on day one.
