Digital guide for the foldable MOLLE chest phone panel. Scan the QR for Field Capture docs — how crews film the shot list.

ATMOSPHERETEAM.COM/DOC-FIELD

  1. Weave the panel onto a vest or chest harness (MOLLE / PALS).
  2. Seat a phone about 4.7–6.7" in the cradle; pull the bungee across.
  3. Open Field Capture and walk the job’s shot list hands-free.
  4. Questions or returns: atmosphereteam.com/contact
