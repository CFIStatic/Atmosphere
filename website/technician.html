What it does

Three tools, zero typing

Nobody documents a loss with their thumbs. The technician app captures the job the way you already move through it.

Record

Audio and video, from the walk-through

Start recording and do the job. The walk-through, the readings you call out, the homeowner's answers — captured once, attached to the job, never retyped.

CapturesAudio · video, hands-free
AttachesTo the job, as evidence
Speak

A conversation, not a form

Hold a spoken conversation with an assistant while you work: log a note, ask what the S500 says about a Category 2 loss, get reminded what you haven't measured yet.

HearsYour voice, over the fans
AnswersIn the middle of the work, out loud
See

The camera names what it sees

Point the phone and the app identifies what's in frame — materials, damage, equipment — so the record carries names and counts, not just footage someone has to scrub through later.

SeesMaterials · damage · equipment
WritesNames and counts into the record
Where it goes

Evidence in, estimate out

What the field captures is what the Mitigation Estimator reads — the scan, the photos, the spoken notes. Better capture on Tuesday is a stronger, more complete estimate on Wednesday, with fewer "performed but never billed" surprises.
