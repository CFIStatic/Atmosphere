Guides & recipes

Concrete ways crews use Work Verification — what to film, what to open on the Platform, and what “done” looks like for each situation.

Morning before + evening after

Situation: One crew, one address, a normal service day.

  1. Open the job in Field Capture on arrival.
  2. Film a short before video: wide at the front, then each work line / room you will touch. Speak the readings.
  3. Do the work.
  4. Film an after video the same way — same angles when you can, so the office can compare.
  5. Confirm both uploads leave pending. On the Platform, open Videos: two rows, two previews, door checks, then dictation.

Done: before + after on the job file, checks recorded, narration attached.

Multi-video long job

Situation: Multi-day or multi-area job — one before/after pair is not enough.

  1. Keep the same job open all week. Do not invent a new job per video.
  2. Film a video per meaningful pass: area A before, area A after, area B before, wrap of the whole site, etc.
  3. If signal is weak, let background upload catch up between areas; clear pending before you leave the site.
  4. Office: sort or scan Videos by time. Ask about a specific video when a customer questions one wing of the building.

Done: every pass has its own video row; the job file is the timeline, not a single megaclip.

Partner job link

Situation: A sub or partner needs to film one job without joining your whole org.

  1. On the Platform, open the job and create / copy the job link for that address.
  2. Send the link to the partner. They open Field Capture (or the linked capture flow), brief, film, and upload for that job only.
  3. Their videos land on your job file with the same door checks and custody trail.

Done: one shared job record — not a photo dump in a group text. Prefer a job link over handing out the org join code.

Share with a customer

Situation: Client asks what happened on site, or needs proof before paying.

  1. Open the job on the Evidence Platform. Confirm Videos and dictation look complete.
  2. Share to the customer’s Atmosphere account (account-to-account). Avoid forwarding raw files out of band.
  3. When they view it, the view lands in custody under their name.

Done: customer can replay footage, checks, and narration; you can revoke later and both actions stay on the log.

Dispute replay

Situation: A line item or day of work is questioned weeks later.

  1. Open the job → Videos. Find the video for that day or area (unique title + preview).
  2. Replay the player. Read door-check findings (location, time, hash) and the dictation / coverage against scope.
  3. Open custody: who uploaded, who shared, who viewed.
  4. Share the relevant evidence with the parties who need it — or walk the replay live on a call.

Done: argument ends on the record, not on memory.

Busy multi-crew week

Situation: Three crews, a dozen open addresses.

  1. Office creates (or confirms) one job file per property before the week starts.
  2. Each crew uses Field Capture on their own seat, picks only their job, films the day’s videos.
  3. PM morning scan: Overview / dashboard for pending uploads or readings; drill into jobs that still need an after video.
  4. Do not mix two addresses into one job “to save time” — that breaks custody and customer shares.

Done: one record per property; twelve jobs, not twelve conflicting camera rolls.

Chest Mount hands-free day

Situation: Gloved work, both hands busy, still need continuous proof.

  1. Mount the phone on the Chest Mount ($49.99, ships typically 2–5 business days).
  2. Open the job, start recording, work the day. Speak readings toward the mic.
  3. Stop between major passes if you want cleaner Videos rows; or film longer segments and let the office Ask into the reading.
  4. When uploading on weak Wi‑Fi, keep the phone powered and uploads pending until clear.

Done: same Platform outcome as handheld — videos on the job, checks, dictation — without juggling the phone every five minutes.
