Guides & recipes

Different ways service contractors use Work Verification — the situation, what to film, and what lands on the record.

Field Capture

The two-minute before clip

Start recording at the door. Anchor the address, walk the shot list from the scope, call out what you are about to work on. The clip is checked at upload — location, time, hash — before anyone watches it.

The after clip at end of day

Film what changed against the shot list. Both clips land on the job with a chain of custody behind them.

Partner contractor on a shared job link

Send a partner their job link. They film their own before-and-after from their phone. Their proof and your certainty are one record — shared account-to-account, not a photo folder.

Evidence Platform

Hand the customer the file

When a client asks what happened on a Tuesday in August, share the verified record — footage, narrated reading against the scope, and every view logged under a name.

Answer a dispute with a replay

A customer questions a line item six weeks later. Open the custody log, replay the clip that day, and send what was filmed on site, what was checked at the door, and what the assistant read.

Invoice with proof attached

Before the invoice goes out, the Evidence Platform shows coverage computed from the scope — not claimed from memory. The PM accepts the reading; payment moves on that acceptance.

Every trade

Busy week across multiple sites

Three crews, twelve open jobs. Each crew films two clips a day per address. The office opens one record per property — not twelve conflicting photo folders.

Before-and-after documentation

Film the starting condition in the before clip, film the finished work in the after. The narrated report reads both against the scope without someone scrubbing video at midnight.
