Field Capture

How to use the phone app on site: open a job, film videos (as many as the day needs), upload in the background, and optionally keep the phone on a Chest Mount. AI reading stays in the office.

What Field Capture is

Field Capture is the crew’s camera for Atmosphere Work Verification. It runs on the phone already in the truck. You join your organization, pick a job, film videos, and upload them to the Evidence Platform. Included in every plan — see pricing.

Prefer the customer word video when you talk about what you film. “Clip” is fine when it matches how the rest of the site talks.

Open the app and find your jobs

  1. Open Field Capture and sign in with the same email and password as the office Platform.
  2. Confirm you are in the right organization (the one that invited this email).
  3. You should see a jobs list — open jobs your org assigned, or a job you opened from a partner job link.
  4. Tap the job you are standing on. Everything you film next attaches to that job file.
If the jobs list is empty, you may be in the wrong org, the job was not created yet on the Platform, or you only have a job-link invite for a different address. Check with the office before filming “somewhere.”

Start recording

Grant camera and microphone (and location when prompted). Start recording when you are ready to walk the work — not from the cab unless the job starts at the curb.

Filming tips that make office reading useful:

  • Start wide — front of the property or unit so the address context is obvious.
  • Walk the work lines — each room, unit, or scope line you are about to touch (or just finished).
  • Speak readings out loud — model numbers, pressures, serials, “exclusion left untouched.” The mic is part of the record.
  • Steady and lit enough — the office needs to see labels and finishes; dark blur helps no one.

Stop and upload

Stop when that pass is done. Upload attaches the video to the job on the Evidence Platform. Uploads can continue in the background while you keep working — you do not need to stare at a progress bar on every file.

Watch for a pending / uploading state if the site has weak signal. Leave the app open (or follow the on-screen guidance) until pending uploads clear, or finish them when you have better connectivity before you leave for the day.

Multiple videos, same job, same day

You are not limited to one before and one after. Film as many videos as the job needs on the same day:

  • Morning before / condition video
  • Midday progress or a second area
  • Evening after / wrap video
  • A short reading close-up you forgot on the first pass

Each video is its own file with its own title and preview on the Platform Videos list. Do not overwrite or “replace” an earlier video by filming again — just record another one.

Chest Mount (optional)

If you want both hands on the work, Atmosphere sells and ships a Field Capture Chest Mount — a foldable MOLLE panel for $49.99, typically 2–5 business days. Optional hardware. The subscription is still what films and holds the record; the mount only keeps the phone on your vest.

What not to expect on the phone

  • No AI dictation or scope coverage on the truck. Reading, narration, Ask, and coverage against scope run in the Evidence Platform after upload.
  • No customer share UI on Field Capture. Shares and custody live in the office.
  • Door checks are not a live pass/fail game while you film. Location, time, and hash checks run when the video arrives at the Platform.

When you are done filming, the office opens the job on the Platform — see the Evidence Platform how-to.
