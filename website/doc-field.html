Field Capture

Film daily work on site — record, speak, and let the camera name what it sees. Built for service contractors.

Built for the job site

Field Capture runs on the phone already in the truck, built for gloved hands and loud rooms: three tools, zero typing. Included in the Work Verification subscription — see pricing.

Record

Film the before clip in the morning and the after clip at end of day. Anchor the address, walk the shot list from the scope, call out readings — captured as video, attached to the job, never retyped.

Speak

Hold a spoken conversation with an assistant while you work: log a note, ask what the scope requires for this step, get reminded what you haven't filmed yet. It answers out loud, over the fans.

See

Point the camera and it names what is in frame — materials, damage, equipment — so the record carries names and counts, not just footage someone has to scrub through later.

Where your clips go

What you film flows into the Evidence Platform — checked at the door for location and time, read against the agreed scope, and held in a chain of custody. Better capture on Tuesday is a settled question eighteen months from now.
