Why for Field Capture

Hands on the work. Phone on the record.

Nobody documents a job site with their thumbs — and they shouldn't have to pinch a phone between glove and tool either. The Chest Mount keeps the camera on your chest, pointed at the work, so Field Capture can take the before-and-after while you run the call.

Film without staging a shot

Walk the shot list the way you already walk the job. The panel holds a modern phone at chest height so the clip is the walk-through, not a one-handed selfie of the ceiling.

Vest or harness, not a tripod

MOLLE / PALS webbing clips to the chest rig most techs already wear. Fold the viewing panel when you need the screen; flatten it when you need both hands in a tight mechanical room.

An accessory, not a second product

Atmosphere sells and ships this as a Field Capture kit. It does not replace Work Verification — it just keeps the phone out of your hands while you film the proof that system holds.

Questions

The ones that decide the order

What's in the box?

(1) the foldable MOLLE chest phone mount, (2) retention straps / bungee, and (3) a digital setup guide emailed after purchase. We do not currently pack a printed card — print the setup card if you want one with the kit.

How long does shipping take?

Typically 2–5 business days from when we confirm the order. We email when it leaves. Fleet quantities may take a day longer to pack — say so in the order note.

Will it fit my phone?

The cradle is cut for most modern smartphones roughly 4.7–6.7 inches, including a typical protective case. Unusual cases, wallets, or oversize batteries may not seat — write us if you are unsure.

What about returns?

Contact us and we will handle fit issues and returns. Do not start with a chargeback — a person replies, usually within one business day.

Does this include Field Capture or Work Verification?

No. This is optional hardware. Field Capture and the Evidence Platform are the software, sold together as Work Verification. The mount just holds the phone while you film.

Who makes the panel?

Atmosphere sells this as a Field Capture accessory. We source a foldable MOLLE chest phone panel, brand the kit, and ship it. We do not claim Atmosphere manufactures the hardware.

The mount holds the phone. The system holds the proof.
