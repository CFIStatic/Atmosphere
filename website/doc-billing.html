Billing & the flat rate

Work Verification at $599.98/month — Field Capture and the Evidence Platform bundled — plus how the one flat token rate is set and metered.

One subscription

Work Verification ships as one monthly subscription at $599.98, plus usage at the flat token rate. The fee includes both parts of the system:

PartWhat it does
Field CaptureOn-site capture — film, voice, and camera — and verification as work happens
Evidence PlatformThe full record — Verifier, audit trail, durable storage, replay, and sharing

Both are required for the system to work, so they are not sold separately.

The flat rate

Usage is billed per token at one simple rate:

Input   $30  per million tokens
Output  $150 per million tokens

Every token is billed at that rate whichever model actually ran the task — your job always goes to the model that does it best, and the bill stays two numbers you can check against the token counts in your audit trail.

Credits: prepaid, always

Usage is prepaid. You buy credits — one credit is one dollar of usage at the flat rate — and agents draw the balance down as they work. Nothing ever runs on an unpaid balance, which is also why there is never a surprise bill: what you loaded is the most you can spend.

Credits come in packs from $10 to $1,000, and the larger packs carry a volume bonus ($100 buys $103 of usage; $1,000 buys $1,075).

Auto-reload or manual reload

In Settings → Billing you choose how the balance stays topped up:

  • Auto-reload on — set a threshold and an amount; when the balance dips below the threshold, a reload purchases itself. The defaults are a $25 reload when the balance falls under $5.
  • Auto-reload off (manual) — every purchase is one you made. When the balance reaches zero, new runs pause and resume the moment you top up; nothing in your record is lost while paused.

Either way, an organization-level monthly spend cap is available, and no reload — automatic or manual — will carry usage past it.

How usage is counted

Token counts come from the model provider's own response, never from the browser — what you are billed is what was actually spent. Every grant, charge, and adjustment lands in an append-only ledger you can read by run, by agent, and by platform.

There is no bundled allowance

Products include no free usage — the monthly fee buys access, and every token comes out of credits you loaded. A quiet week costs you the subscription and nothing more; unused credits just wait.
