Three ways in

01

Bring your team

Create an organization at sign-up, pick your role and kind of work, then share the join code. Everyone who enters it lands in your workspace.

02

Connect a site

Link a carrier portal or supplier site once. From then on, hand Atmosphere work that lives inside it — every step recorded.

03

Run the agent

One Anthropic API key and one command put an agent at a real computer — macOS, Windows, or Linux — connected outbound-only.
