Manager Platform

Run the business

Every job leaves numbers behind. The Manager Platform is where those numbers stop being a shoebox: job costing while the job is still open, the accounting work that eats an office manager's week, and the insight to see which work is actually worth taking.

JOB COSTING ACCOUNTING BUSINESS INSIGHTS
Job costing

Know the margin before the job closes

A job that lost money tells you on the day it closes, which is far too late to do anything about it. Costing runs against the live job instead.

Live

Cost against the estimate, as it happens

Labor logged, equipment placed, materials bought, subs invoiced — set against what the estimate said they would be, while the job is still open and there is still something to be done about it.

TracksLabor · equipment · materials · subcontractors
ComparesActual against the estimate, per line
FlagsA job crossing its margin — while it is open
Terms

Your margin, O&P, and tax assumptions

Costing uses the same per-organization margin, overhead and profit, tax and cost-basis assumptions the estimators price from — so the estimate and the cost report are never two different opinions.

SharesOne set of assumptions with the estimators
HoldsMargin · O&P · tax · cost basis, per organization
MeansThe bid and the books agree
Accounting

The week the office manager gets back

Not a general ledger — the work that surrounds one. Getting the numbers out of the jobs, into the invoices, and into whatever system already holds your books.

Billing

Invoices built from the job, not retyped from it

The estimate, the change orders, and what was actually performed are already in the record. An invoice is a reading of that record — and it will not go out with a blocking document still missing.

BuildsInvoices from estimate · change orders · work performed
BlocksBilling with required documentation outstanding
TracksSent · aged · paid, per job
Reconcile

The numbers, moved for you

Agents that already sign in to carrier portals and supplier sites can do the same for the systems your books live in — pulling the numbers out, entering them in, and leaving a receipt for every step.

PullsFrom the systems already holding your data
EntersInto your accounting software, step by step
ProvesEvery entry — in the audit trail
Business insights

Which work is worth taking

A year of jobs already knows which carriers pay, which crews finish, and which kind of loss quietly loses money. The platform reads what you already have.

Margin

Margin by job type, by carrier, by crew, by month. Not a dashboard for its own sake — the specific answer to which work to chase and which to stop bidding.

Breaks downJob type · carrier · crew · period
