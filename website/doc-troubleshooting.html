Troubleshooting

When something misbehaves — sign-in, join codes, Field Capture camera/GPS/upload, Videos list quirks, seats and payment — start here.

Signing in

My password isn’t working

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. A reset link arrives by email; the token exchange happens on the server. If the link expired, request a fresh one rather than retrying it.

My PIN doesn’t work on this phone

That is the PIN doing its job: it is bound to the single device you enrolled it on. On any other device, sign in with your password, then enroll a PIN there too if you want one.

Organizations and join codes

The join code is rejected

Codes are exact — check for a mistyped character first. If it still fails, the code may have been rotated; ask whoever runs your organization to read it from Settings.

I created a second organization by mistake

Sign out, sign back in, and join the right organization with its code. Ask us via the contact page to remove the empty one.

Do not post the org join code publicly. Prefer a single-job partner link when an outside crew only needs one address.

Field Capture

Camera or microphone won’t start

  1. Check OS permissions for camera and mic for Field Capture (or the browser, if you are on a web capture link).
  2. Close other apps that hold the camera.
  3. Restart the app and open the job again before recording.

Location / GPS looks wrong

Door checks compare capture location to the property. Enable location for the app, wait for a fix outdoors if you can, and avoid filming the “before” from miles away in the yard of a different job. If GPS was off during capture, say so when you contact Support — the finding is still recorded honestly.

Upload stuck or pending forever

  1. Confirm network (try site Wi‑Fi or a stable phone hotspot).
  2. Leave Field Capture open until pending uploads clear; background upload still needs a chance to finish.
  3. Do not delete the local video until the Platform Videos list shows it.
  4. If it still stalls, note the job id and retry when you have better signal — then contact Support with that job id.

Evidence Platform — Videos

A video is missing

Confirm the crew filmed against the correct job and that upload left pending on the phone. Hard-refresh the Platform. Check Overview for processing items. If the phone still shows a failed upload, finish it there first.

Two videos show the same title or the same preview

Hard-refresh first. Each upload should be its own row with a unique title and matching preview. If duplicates persist after refresh, contact Support with the job id and both video ids — do not re-film over the “wrong” one until we confirm what landed.

Dictation or Ask hasn’t appeared

Door checks run first; reading follows. Large videos take longer. If checks are done and reading never starts, send the job id and video id via Support. AI usage for completed reading is billed the day it runs — see Billing & usage.

Billing and seats

I can’t invite another Field Capture user

You may be at the seat limit for your plan (Starter 1 · Work Verification 3 · Scale 10). Invites past the included seats add a seat at $125/mo, or upgrade the plan. Open Usage / Billing in the Platform to see included vs. extra seats and the payment method on file.

A charge looks wrong

Plans, extra seats, and same-day AI usage invoices settle through Stripe. Open Usage for receipts. If an invoice and what you see in Usage disagree, send us both — that is a bug worth a human.

Prepaid credit packs are not the current path for Work Verification. If you still see a legacy credit balance somewhere, it is not how new usage bills; contact us rather than buying packs to “unpause agents.”

Still stuck?

Write to us with the job id and video id when the problem involves footage or a job file. That lets us open the same record you see — much more useful than a screenshot alone.
