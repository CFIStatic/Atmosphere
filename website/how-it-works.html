How it works

From two videos a day to a record you can hand to a customer

Work Verification is a pipeline, not a feature list. Crews film against a shot list. The platform checks integrity at the door, reads the footage against the agreed scope, seals it into an append-only chain of custody, and shares it account-to-account with whoever needs to release the money. This page walks through every stage.

OVERVIEW CAPTURE INTEGRITY READING CUSTODY SHARING THE RULES WHO USES IT
Overview

Four stages, every day, on every job

The crew's cost is two short videos — before and after. Everything else happens to the footage, not to the crew.

01

Film it like this

A shot list built from the job's own scope: start wide at the front, walk each line of work, film the exclusions untouched. Thirty seconds of instructions, not a training course.

02

Checked at the door

GPS puts the phone on the site. The clock puts it on the day. The hash proves it was never re-cut. A clip filmed two miles away is flagged before anybody watches it.

03

Read by the assistant

AI reads the footage against the agreed scope, watches the stage of work live while filming, and writes a narrated report — what was done, what was covered, what it could not see.

04

Held on the record

Sealed, labeled, and entered into an append-only chain of custody. Every view, share and download afterward is an entry under a verified name.

Capture

The shot list comes from the scope

Before filming, the crew sees the day's guide: the anchor shot that proves the address, a walk of every included line, and the excluded work filmed untouched — because "we never touched the skylights" is worth more with footage of the skylights. The guide is built from what both sides already agreed to, so filming it is agreeing to it again, on camera.

Capture runs through Field Capture — the technician app on site. The crew does not learn a new workflow; they follow the list, record two clips, and upload when they have signal. The rest of the pipeline starts the moment the clip arrives.

Integrity

Checked at the door, before meaning is read

Before the footage is read for content, it is checked as evidence: filmed on site, filmed on the day claimed, never filed before, never altered since. The checks are stored as findings from the moment of upload — what was known when somebody decided, not what today's code would conclude. Only then does the AI read what the work actually shows.

A verification platform is only worth what it refuses to accept. Clips that fail location or time checks are flagged visibly. Unchecked reads as unverified — neither pass nor fail — because "we could not tell" must always be an answer the system can give.

Reading

A narrated report on every clip

The assistant watches the stage of work live while the crew films — anchor, scope, wrap — and afterward writes the day's report: what the footage shows, step by step, with coverage computed against the shot list rather than claimed. "Covered 4 of 5 steps — missing: the far bedroom" is a sentence a project manager can act on before the crew leaves the driveway.

The reading is not a sign-off. It is one claim in the record — rendered differently from a person's acceptance everywhere in the product. Money moves on human acceptance, never silently on the model's output alone.

Custody

Held on the record — append-only, sealed, citable

Once integrity checks and the reading are attached, the clip enters the chain of custody. The hash is fixed. The findings are fixed. The custody log is append-only: nothing edits history, including whoever owns the job. Eighteen months later, the disputed Tuesday is still footage with a location, a timestamp, a reading, and every access since — not two memories of a phone call.

Sealed at arrival

SHA-256 fingerprinted the moment the clip lands. Any re-cut or re-upload is a new object with a new entry.

Labeled honestly

Before, after, stage of work, coverage gaps — every label ties back to what the footage actually showed.

Nothing deletes

Revocation, correction, and dispute all add entries. The log outranks its owner.

Sharing

Hand the record to the person who releases the money

Customers, reviewers, and attorneys review evidence in the Verifier — a records screen, not an operations console. Shares are account-to-account: issued to a named person's email, opening only for an Atmosphere account signed in with it. Forwarded, the link refuses — which is what puts a verified name on every custody entry instead of "someone with the link".

Watching is free

The reviewer sees every clip on the job, the checks, the analysis and the custody trail. Every view lands in the log under their name.

Keeping a copy is the paid act

A download settles your organization's fee first, through an append-once ledger. No signed file leaves until the ledger reads paid or waived.

Revocation is immediate

A share dies the moment you revoke it, and both the share and the revocation are custody entries. Who could see what, and when, is always a query.

The rules

Three rules that make it evidence, not footage

These refusals are structural — enforced by the database and the screens, not by policy.

unknown ≠ pass

Unchecked is not clean

A clip with no location reads as unverified, styled as neither pass nor fail. "We could not tell" is an answer the system is required to be able to give.

AI ≠ acceptance

A reading is not a sign-off

The model's reading and a person's acceptance are different claims, rendered differently everywhere. Money moves on the second, never silently on the first.

append-only

The log outranks its owner

Nothing in the chain of custody can be edited or removed — including by whoever owns the job. A custody record the custodian can change is not a custody record.

Who uses it

Both sides of every check that changes hands

Verification is not surveillance of one side by the other. The crew's proof and the customer's certainty are the same file.

Service contractor

Pay on footage, not on faith

"Can I release Tuesday's draw" is answered by Tuesday's clips, read against the scope, before the crew has left the site.

Field crew

The day you worked is provable forever

Two videos a day, and no more arguing about what state the site was in when you arrived — or what you never touched.

Customer & reviewer

Settle from the record

Every clip, its integrity verdicts and its narrated report, on one screen — with the chain of custody that makes it citable.

Attorney & mediator

Read the job back

Eighteen months later, the disputed Tuesday is footage with a hash, a location, a reading and every access since — not two memories.
