# Legal requests (legal@atmosphereteam.com)

Users, customers, law enforcement and other third parties send requests for
videos, records or evidence to **legal@atmosphereteam.com**. Cloudflare Email
Routing forwards it to Jack (rule "legal@ -> jack (legal requests)").

Public text lives in `website/legal.html`, `website/privacy.html#legal-requests`
and `website/terms.html#legal-requests` (section 15).

What we say publicly (keep it in sync, and do not add response-time promises):

- Law enforcement and third parties need valid legal process (subpoena, court
  order or warrant, as applicable). An email alone is not enough to disclose.
- Each request is reviewed by a person.
- We may notify the affected customer unless legally prohibited.
- We can place a legal hold to preserve evidence while a request is reviewed.
  How holds work: [legal-hold.md](legal-hold.md).
- Emergencies (imminent risk of death or serious physical injury) get
  expedited review; subject line "EMERGENCY".
- Individuals wanting job evidence go to the workspace owner (the Customer owns
  job records); requests about their own personal data go to legal@.
